Studio › Products. A product belongs to one brand. What orders use today: the order price (unit price on order lines), the VAT, “Strategic” (counts in the strategic share that picks the pack discount), “Pack discount applies” (the pack % is applied to this product — old “Rémunérer”) and the free-unit options (UG) offered on an order line. The other prices (distributor, wholesaler, pharmacy, discounted) are kept for information. A product is a big record: list → reading panel → full page.
