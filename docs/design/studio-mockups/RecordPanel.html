Record panel, redesigned for long records (example: an expense setting). Today the panel is one long form: every field is editable at once, and a quick look means scrolling through 13 inputs. Proposal: ① open in reading mode (the key numbers first, grouped in sections), ② edit one section at a time, ③ add with short steps. Simple records (a channel, a region) keep today's short form.

① Open a setting: reading mode

The answer to “what does this rep get?” is visible at once: the 4 prices in big numbers. Details are grouped in short sections. Nothing is editable until you choose a section. Delete moves to the ⋯ menu so it is never next to Save.
