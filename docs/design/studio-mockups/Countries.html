Good to know

  • Countries are read-only for a lab (the central console assigns them): on this page “Add”, “Save” and “Delete” are hidden and the fields can't be edited. The other 4 levels use the full version shown here.
  • The level strip (Countries · Regions · Wilayas · Communes · Zones) moves between the 5 geography pages without going back to Settings.
  • “Regions in Algeria” lists the children; each one opens the Regions page already filtered on that country.
  • Delete asks for confirmation and is blocked with a clear message when children still exist.
