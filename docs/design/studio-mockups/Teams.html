Studio › Teams. A team groups people who promote the same brands to the same specialities (Cardio, Neuro, Pharma Est…). Teams are used by rules, expense settings, objectives, campaigns and surveys to say “who”. Same pattern as the other Studio pages: list, then a side panel to read a team and edit it section by section. Membership keeps its history: removing someone ends their stint on a date, it is never deleted (past reports keep the right team) — the same timeline as Users › Assignments.
