Team › Expenses — proposal. A rep sends one claim per working day: the trips of the day (from → to, km), where they slept, and other costs with receipts. Delorc computes the amount from the rep's expense setting (Studio › Expense settings): km × rate, capped per day, plus a meal or a night. A manager reviews by rep and by month and approves days one by one or all at once; the amount is kept when approved. Other expenses and Trips leave the menu: they are now inside each day. Rules to approve are at the bottom.

Example setting used on this page (Karim, VM · Cardio): 15 DA/km in zone, 20 DA/km out of zone, max 150 km in / 400 km out a day, meal 900 DA, night 4,500 DA, max 8 nights a month, max 2 meals on a night away.

Rules to approve

  1. One claim = one working day of one rep. No claim on a weekend, a holiday or a day already claimed. decidedExample: Karim cannot add Fri 9 Oct (weekend) nor a second claim for 14 Oct; he edits the one he has.
  2. Each trip is “in zone” or “out of zone”. Prefilled from the rep's territory: a commune in a Hors résidence wilaya → out; Résidence or Semi-résidence → in. The rep can change it. In-zone km are paid at the in rate up to max km in; out-of-zone km at the out rate up to max km out, each cap per day. decidedExample: 14 Oct, Alger → Tizi Ouzou → Alger (Semi-résidence), 210 km in → 150 paid × 15 = 2,250 DA. 12 Oct, Oran → Alger, 430 km out → 400 × 20 = 8,000 DA, although he sleeps at home that night (so: + meal).
  3. Where the rep slept + how many meals. The rep picks At home or Away and types the number of meals they paid. At home: up to 1 meal paid. Away: the night + up to “max meals on a night away” (new box in the expense setting, 2 by default). Meals above the limit are shown but not paid. Max nights counts per calendar month, in date order, over waiting and approved days (refused days don't count); past it, the day pays its km and meals, no night. decidedExample: Sun 11 Oct in Oran, 3 meals typed → night 4,500 + 2 × 900 = 6,300 DA (+ km), the 3rd meal shows “over the limit”. Samir's 9th night of October: km + meals, no night.
  4. Data: on expenses, column type gets clear values home / away (today meal / out; existing rows converted) and a new column meals (number typed, existing rows get 1). On expense_settings, new column max_meals_per_night (existing settings get 2). decided
  5. Other costs = an expense type (Studio › Expense types) + an amount + an optional receipt, paid as typed (tolls, parking, fuel…). No cap. decided
  6. Which setting applies: the one in force for that rep on that day (already the case: personal → role + team → role → team → everyone). Changing the day picks the setting again.
  7. Statuses: Waiting → Approved or Refused (reason required). The rep can edit or delete a day only while it is waiting or refused; editing a refused day sends it again (back to waiting). An approved day is locked. decided
  8. Who approves: new right expenses.approve, for the reps the person manages (all levels below) — never their own claims. Admin approves everyone. decided
  9. Amount kept on approval: while waiting, the amount is computed live; on approval it is saved (new columns on expenses), so later price changes never touch approved days. decided
  10. Who sees what: a rep sees their own claims; a manager sees the reps below them and themself; admin sees all. decided
  11. Adding expenses is its own page, several days at once: one block per day with its date, its trips (from a commune to a commune, wilaya first), its night and meals (no night · meals only, or night away · meals) and its costs. On Send, each block becomes one day claim (as before: approved or refused on its own). Editing opens one day. to approve
  12. Not now: km computed from a map (the rep types km, like the old apps), linking a day to its visits (Visits module), a second approval level, payment tracking (“paid on…”).
