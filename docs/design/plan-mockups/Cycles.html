Plan › Cycles — proposal. A cycle is one person's working period (a month by default): the professionals they must see, each with a segment and a number of visits (from Frequencies), and their targets for the period (from Objectives). Managers create cycles for many people at once, follow progress in the list, and open a cycle in a side panel or as a full page. The old pages “Cycles”, “Cycle targets” and the cycle detail page are merged into this one module. Rules to approve are listed at the bottom (to approve).

Rules to approve

  1. Cycle state comes from the dates only: Upcoming · Running · Ended. No “submitted / approved” step (the old apps had the words but nothing used them). to approveExample: cycle 1 → 31 Oct, today 30 Oct → Running; from 1 Nov → Ended.
  2. One cycle at a time per person (already enforced by the database). Dates can be changed while no visit falls outside the new dates.Example: Karim has a visit on 28 Oct → his October cycle can't end on 25 Oct.
  3. Each role says which kinds of professionals it visits (new “Visits” setting in Studio › Roles), used to fill cycles. Replaces the old hard-coded VM/VP. to approveExample: Medical rep → doctors, dentists, pharmacies; Pharma rep → pharmacies, wholesalers. A Pharma rep in “Alger Cardio” gets Pharmacie Belkacem, not Dr Kaci.
  4. A new cycle is filled from the previous cycle + the territory (step 3, can be changed each time). Segments are copied from the previous cycle; newcomers have no segment. to approveExample: Dr Kaci was A in October → A in November. Dr Merabet just joined Alger Est → in November with no segment, 0 visits to do, flagged.
  5. Visits to do = Frequencies grid for the cycle owner's role + main team, the professional's speciality and segment; the speciality row falls back to “Every other kind” (pharmacies always use it). Today's table needs a speciality on every row, so “Every other kind” = an empty speciality. to approveExample: Medical rep · Cardio, Pharmacie Belkacem, segment B → “Every other kind” B = 1 visit.
  6. Figures: Coverage = professionals seen at least once ÷ professionals in the cycle. Frequency reached = Σ min(done, to do) ÷ Σ to do. Extra visits = Σ max(done − to do, 0). Expected by today = goal × working days passed ÷ working days. Only completed visits count; planned ones are shown apart.Example: Dr Saïdi, C = 1 visit, seen 2 times → 1 counts toward “frequency reached”, 1 is extra.
  7. Removing a professional who already has visits in the cycle is refused (the visits would lose their link); professionals no longer in the territory stay, flagged by “Check territory”.
  8. Later, not in this module: reps proposing changes to their list with manager approval (with the rep mobile app, like HCP change requests), Objectives page redesign (next module), alerts by e-mail.
