Plan › Objectives and Frequencies — proposal. These two settings give a cycle its numbers. Objectives say how much a person must do a day (by role and team). Frequencies say how many times each professional must be visited per cycle (by role, team, speciality and segment). Both keep their history: a change applies “from a date”, so cycles already started keep their old numbers. Frame 1 shows how they meet in a cycle. Rules to approve are at the bottom.

1 · How a cycle gets its numbers

Example: Karim Bensaïd, role Medical rep, main team Cardio, territory Alger Est. His November cycle (1 → 30 Nov, 21 working days).

Objectives Medical rep · Cardio

A day: 10 doctor visits · 4 pharmacy visits · 1 order
10 × 21 days = 210 doctor visits
4 × 21 days = 84 pharmacy visits
1 × 21 days = 21 orders
→ the cycle's targets for the period: what he must do in total.
→

His November cycle

Goal 294 visits (210 + 84) · 21 orders Professionals 218 (from October + territory) Visits needed by the list 301 (sum of each one's number)
301 needed ≥ 294 goal → ✓ the list is big enough
←

Frequencies Medical rep · Cardio

Each professional's number of visits in the cycle:
Dr Kaci · Cardiologie · A → 3
Dr Saïdi · Méd. générale · C → 1
Pharmacie Belkacem · B → 1 (every other kind)
→ adds up to the visits needed by the list.
The two meet in the cycle: Objectives = how much work he must do; Frequencies = how that work is spread over his professionals. If the list needs fewer visits than the goal (e.g. 180 needed for a 294 goal), the cycle page warns: “The list is too small for the goal — add professionals or raise segments.” If it needs far more, some professionals won't be seen.

Rules to approve

  1. Objective numbers are “a day”, and a cycle's targets = number × its working days (lab weekend and holidays out). Same as the old apps and today's API. to approveExample: 10 doctor visits a day, November = 21 working days → 210 doctor visits for the cycle.
  2. The numbers: doctor visits (doctors + dentists), pharmacy visits, wholesaler visits (wholesalers + distributors), orders, coaching visits. Today's API has doctor, pharmacy (“business”), orders and coaching; wholesaler visits is new (Scipharma's Pharma reps had 2 a day). The old “public / private” numbers of Inphamedis are not kept. to approveExample: Pharma rep · All teams = 12 pharmacy + 2 wholesaler visits a day → goal 14 visits a day.
  3. Coaching visits a day is for people who coach (supervisors): visits they make together with a rep. For them it is the daily goal, like in the old apps. to approveExample: Supervisor · All teams = 12 coaching visits a day → 252 for November.
  4. Team can be “All teams”. For a person, Delorch takes the row of their role + main team, else the row of their role + “All teams”, else nothing (listed under “People with no objective”). Same for Frequencies grids. to approveExample: Karim (Medical rep, Cardio) → “Medical rep · Cardio”. Yacine (Medical rep, team Hôpitaux, no row) → “Medical rep · All other teams”. Omar (Directeur régional) → nothing.
  5. Changes apply from a date; the old numbers end the day before and stay in the history. Cycles that start on or after that date take the new numbers (including ones already created); older cycles keep theirs. A row used by a cycle can't be deleted. One current row per role + team. to approveExample: change to 12 doctor visits from 1 Dec → December cycles = 12 × days; the November cycle stays at 10 × days.
  6. Frequencies: speciality row, else “Every other kind” (a row with no speciality); empty cell = 0. Pharmacies always use “Every other kind”. to approveExample: Medical rep · Cardio, Dr Lounis (Pneumologie, no row), segment A → “Every other kind” A = 2 visits.
  7. A doctor's speciality is the one on their current workplace; with two current workplaces, the one that started most recently. to approve
  8. The cycle checks the list against the goal: visits needed by the list (sum of each professional's number) vs the goal. Shown on the cycle page, warned when the list is too small. to approveExample: 218 professionals needing 301 visits, goal 294 → OK. 150 professionals needing 180 → “list too small for the goal”.
