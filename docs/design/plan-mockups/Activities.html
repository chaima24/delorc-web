Plan › Activities — proposal. An activity is what a person does that is not a visit: training, meeting, congress, leave, sick day… Its type (Studio › Field setup › Activity types) says what kind of day it is — Field day Off-field work day Non-working day — and what it can be linked to (professionals, a territory, reps). Off-field and non-working days lower the cycle's goal (fewer field days) and explain the days with no visit. Managers see a list and a team calendar; a side panel to read, edit or delete; “Add activity” in a side panel. Rules to approve are at the bottom.

Rules to approve

  1. Each activity belongs to one person (“For”): whose day it is. Today the API only keeps who created it, so a manager adding a leave for Karim would make it the manager's leave. New: one column user_id on activities (empty = the creator, so existing rows keep working). to approveExample: Nadia (manager) adds “Annual leave” for Karim → it is Karim's leave, “Added by Nadia”.
  2. An activity linked to reps covers them too. to approveExample: “Cycle meeting”, morning of 26 Oct, for Nadia, linked to Karim, Samir, Lina, Rania → each of the 5 gets ½ off-field day on 26 Oct.
  3. How it counts in a cycle (approved with Cycles): off-field and non-working days lower the field days; a field day (e.g. a round-table with doctors) does not. Morning or afternoon = ½ day; all day = 1; “2 to 5 days” = that many working days (weekend and holidays are skipped). A day counts once even with two activities. to approveExample: “Training, 3 days” from Thu 8 Oct (weekend Fri–Sat) covers Thu 8, Sun 11, Mon 12 — not Fri and Sat.
  4. Which types a person can pick = the types allowed for their role (already set in Studio › Activity types).
  5. Who sees and changes them: people with the Activities permission see all activities (like professionals — no hierarchy rule). Anyone with the right can edit or delete; the cycle's figures follow at once. to approve
  6. Not now: approval of activities by the manager (the old apps had a status but never used it), budgets and guests of marketing events (Campaigns module).
