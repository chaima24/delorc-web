Central › Labs › a lab › Rule types (module 6a). The building blocks of the lab's territories (region, wilaya, commune, speciality, segmentation). They live in the lab's database; central adds, edits and deletes them lab by lab, with every field, as before. A new lab gets the five types when it is created. The lab only reads them (Studio). A type a territory uses can't be hidden or deleted. Type names are the real seeded ones; counts, territories and people are invented. Dashed amber boxes are points to confirm.
