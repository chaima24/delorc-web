Every existing feature keeps its place. Only the way you reach it changes.

Six UX principles

Panels, not modals Records open in a side panel and the list stays visible. One shared Sheet component replaces about 118 hand-built overlays.
⌘K to go anywhere One bar to find a doctor, start a visit, log an expense or open a cycle. Results are filtered by your permissions.
The cycle is always visible Current cycle, days left and coverage sit in the top bar on every screen. Reps and managers always know where they stand.
Status = word + shape + color Planned ○, In progress ◐, Completed ●, Rejected ✕. Readable in grayscale, in Arabic and in sunlight.
RTL by construction Only logical spacing (start / end). Arabic gets its own type rhythm, not a mirrored afterthought.
Thumb-first in the field Visit, e-ADV, samples and order fit in one flow. Every action is at the bottom of the screen and at least 48 px tall.
